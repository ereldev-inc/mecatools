About this tool

The DOT code is molded on the tire sidewall. Its last four digits give the manufacturing date: the first two are the week (01 to 53) and the last two the year. For example 2319 means week 23 of 2019. Tires made before 2000 only have three digits.

Rubber ages even when the tire is not used. Tires are commonly inspected more closely after about 5 years and replaced at 10 years at the latest, regardless of wear. Always follow the advice of the tire manufacturer, and check for cracks, hardening and deformations.
